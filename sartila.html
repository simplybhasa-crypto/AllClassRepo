More than a team

A club with a big story.

Real Madrid Club de Fútbol is one of the world's best-known football clubs. Founded in 1902, the team plays in white and represents Spain's capital city. Its supporters are known around the world as Madridistas.

The Santiago Bernabéu Stadium is the club's home. Match days there bring together generations of fans to cheer on their team. Real Madrid has built a long history in Spanish and European football, with famous players and unforgettable matches across many eras.

A few dates to remember

Milestones & memories

  1. 1902

    The club begins

    Madrid Football Club is founded. The title “Real” is granted in 1920.

  2. 1947

    A new home

    The stadium that would become the Santiago Bernabéu opens in Madrid.

  3. 1955

    Europe's new competition

    Real Madrid wins the first European Cup, beginning a remarkable run in the competition.
